Workflow Pipeline

How CardioPulse Analyzes Patient Records

From physiological inputs to soft voting consensus in 5 automated phases.

1

Clinical Inputs

Doctor enters 13 validated patient attributes (BP, cholesterol, ECG, thal, oldpeak).

2

Preprocessing

ColumnTransformer applies median imputation and StandardScaler to continuous values.

3

Multi-Model Run

All 6 individual classification algorithms generate independent probability estimates.

4

Hybrid Ensemble

Soft Voting combines predicted probabilities using calibrated model confidence weights.

5

Clinical Report

Final prediction, confidence gauge, model breakdown, and clinical factors are rendered.
